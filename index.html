RailGuard
Hardware-Based Yellow Line Breach Detection & Platform End Safety System
Institute Of Engineering & Management, Kolkata

The Innovation: Why This Matters?

Current Metro stations rely on manual supervision and reactive CCTV. This system introduces a proactive, automated safety layer to prevent track intrusions and platform edge accidents.

Real-Time Protection

Unlike human observation, the hardware-based detection works continuously with zero downtime or distraction.

Non-Intrusive Design

Requires no drilling, welding, or civil modifications to the platform floor or track area.

Intelligent Operation

System automatically pauses during train boarding and resumes once the train departs, eliminating false alarms.

How It Works

Optical Intrusion Detection: Active IR through-beam sensors form a virtual wall parallel to the safety line.

Sensing Layer

Uses 4 Ultrasonic sensors for train movement measurement and IR sets for line breach detection.

Control Logic

Processed via Raspberry Pi 4 and Arduino Uno using debounce logic to filter out transient disturbances.

Instant Actuation

Triggers isolated relay modules for audio alarms and control room notifications.
