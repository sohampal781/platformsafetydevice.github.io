Future of Transit Safety

Hardware-Based Yellow Line Breach Detection and Platform End Safety System

An autonomous solution leveraging deterministic logic to protect commuters in real-time.

System Characteristics

Real-Time Operation

Immediate detection and alert response times for sub-second protection.

Non-Contact Hardware Detection

Uses specialized proximity and boundary sensors to ensure high industrial reliability.

Continuous Monitoring

The system operates automatically without the need for constant human supervision.

Fail-Safe Output

Opto-isolated relays ensure safety triggers even during power or system faults.

Non-Intrusive Installation

Mechanical clamping interface allows for installation without any civil modifications.

Why We Are Different

Automatic vs. Manual CCTV

Detects breaches automatically, removing the risk of human observation delays.

Instant Actuation

Generates immediate audio-visual alerts to secure the platform instantly.

Low Cost & High Reliability

Economical hardware-based design that works without internet or software dependency.
