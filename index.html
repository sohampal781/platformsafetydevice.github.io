Future of Transit Safety

Hardware-Based Yellow Line Breach Detection and Platform End Safety System

An autonomous, industrial-grade solution leveraging deterministic logic to protect commuters in real-time.

System Characteristics

Real-Time Operation

Continuous platform monitoring with immediate detection and sub-second alert response times.

Non-Contact Detection

Sophisticated IR and ultrasonic sensor arrays eliminate the need for mechanical parts, ensuring zero wear and tear.

Automatic Oversight

A fully autonomous system that maintains 24/7 vigilance without the need for human intervention.

Fail-Safe Architecture

Isolated outputs and deterministic logic ensure safety triggers even during system power interruptions.

Non-Intrusive Design

Mechanical clamping interface allows for seamless installation on existing safety railings without civil modifications.

Why We Are Different

Proactive vs. Reactive

Unlike traditional CCTV which relies on human observation, our system identifies breaches as they happen.

100% Hardware Stability

Operates independently of internet connectivity or software vulnerabilities, ensuring maximum field reliability.

Cost Efficiency

An economical, high-impact solution that avoids expensive infrastructure overhauls or construction.
